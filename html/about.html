Our story

From a two-car garage to seven models.

It started with a rebuilt roadster and a hand-painted sign. Neighbours came to look, then to buy, then to ask when the next one would be ready.

Today our line-up runs from a city hatch to a 760-horsepower electric — but every car still gets a full road test before it reaches you, and every customer still gets the keys from a person, not a portal.

We believe buying a car should feel like the first drive: exciting, clear and a little bit fast.
